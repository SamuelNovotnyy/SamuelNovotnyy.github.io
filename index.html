appliances you can connect

air condition

Control your air conditioning remotely with our smart thermostat, allowing you to adjust the temperature from anywhere using your smartphone or voice commands.

Control your air conditioning remotely with our state-of-the-art smart thermostat. This device allows you to adjust the temperature of your home from anywhere, whether you're at work, on vacation, or just lounging on the couch. Simply use our user-friendly mobile app or give a voice command to your smart home assistant. The smart thermostat can also learn your preferences over time, automatically adjusting settings for optimal comfort and energy efficiency. Installation is straightforward, and the device seamlessly integrates with existing HVAC systems, making it easy for you to stay cool in summer and warm in winter.

security camera

Monitor your property with our smart outdoor cameras, giving you real-time video feeds and alerts on your phone, ensuring your home is safe and secure at all times.

Monitor your property with our advanced smart outdoor cameras, which offer high-definition video feeds and motion detection alerts directly to your smartphone. These cameras are weather-resistant and equipped with night vision, ensuring your home is protected day and night. You can view live footage, review recorded clips, and receive instant notifications if any movement is detected, all through our intuitive mobile app. Installing the cameras is straightforward: mount them in strategic locations around your property, connect them to your Wi-Fi, and follow the app's prompts to complete the setup.

motion sensors

Enhance home security with our motion sensors, which can trigger alerts, activate lighting, or interact with other smart devices to keep your home safe and responsive.

Enhance your home security with our reliable smart motion sensors. These sensors detect movement within their range and send instant notifications to your smartphone, alerting you to any unusual activity. They can be placed in various locations around your home, such as entryways, hallways, or near valuable items. The motion sensors are designed to integrate seamlessly with your smart home system, allowing for automation triggers such as turning on lights or activating cameras when movement is detected. Setup is simple: mount the sensors using the included adhesive or screws, connect them to your home network, and configure them through our app for immediate use.

room lamp

Manage your lamps with our smart lighting system, enabling you to turn lights on or off, dim them, and set schedules using your phone or voice-activated smart home hub.

Manage your lamps with our comprehensive smart lighting system. This system allows you to turn lights on or off, dim them, and set custom schedules to suit your daily routines, all from your smartphone or through voice commands. You can create scenes for different occasions, such as a cozy movie night or a bright morning wake-up. The smart bulbs are easy to install, simply screw them into your existing light fixtures and connect them to your home Wi-Fi. Pairing them with our app or a compatible smart home hub takes just a few minutes, providing you with flexible and dynamic lighting control.

Effortlessly open and close your garage door with our innovative smart garage door opener. This device provides secure and convenient access to your garage through your smartphone or a voice command to your smart home assistant. You can check the status of your garage door in real-time and receive alerts if it's left open, adding an extra layer of security to your home. The setup is simple and typically involves connecting the smart opener to your existing garage door system and linking it to your home Wi-Fi network. Our detailed instructions guide you through the process, ensuring a smooth installation and immediate functionality.
